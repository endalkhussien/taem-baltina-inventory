## 3. What we supply

Taem Baltina is an Ethiopian spice kitchen. We buy raw spices and chickpea flour, mill and blend in small batches, and sell finished pantry spices for everyday wot and for larger kitchens.

The bid is limited to the three products below. We do not bid goods we do not make.

| Product | What it is for | How it is sold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Berbere | The base blend for wot and stews: chili, fenugreek, garlic, ginger, black cumin, and salt. | By the kilogram |
| Shiro | Roasted chickpea flour blended with garlic, ginger, mild pepper, and salt, for shiro wot. | By the kilogram |
| Mitmita | A hot finishing spice of bird’s-eye chili, black cumin, ginger, garlic, and salt, for kitfo and marinades. | By the kilogram |

Recipes stay with the kitchen. The buyer receives the finished blend, the mill date, and the net weight. We do not publish gram-by-gram formulas in a bid.

## 4. Specification

### 4.1 Berbere

- Finished dry blend, milled for kitchen use.
- Principal ingredients: red pepper, fenugreek, garlic, ginger, black cumin, salt.
- Color and aroma consistent within a batch. A new batch may vary slightly with the pepper harvest. We will not ship a batch that is damp, moldy, or off-aroma.
- Unit of sale: 1 kilogram. Larger orders are multiples of 1 kilogram.

### 4.2 Shiro

- Finished dry blend for shiro wot.
- Principal ingredients: chickpea flour, red pepper, garlic, ginger, salt.
- Free of visible foreign matter. No added color.
- Unit of sale: 1 kilogram.

### 4.3 Mitmita

- Finished hot blend.
- Principal ingredients: bird’s-eye chili, black cumin, ginger, garlic, salt.
- Noticeably hotter than the berbere in this bid. It is a finishing spice, not a substitute for berbere.
- Unit of sale: 1 kilogram.

### 4.4 What “acceptable” means

A delivery is acceptable when:

1. The product names and kilograms match the order.
2. Packs are intact, dry, and labeled with product name, net weight in kilograms, and mill date.
3. The spice is free of insects, mold, and foreign material a kitchen would reject on sight or smell.

If a pack fails that test, we replace it or credit its value. The buyer should tell us within 2 working days of delivery and keep the pack for collection.

### 4.5 Use window

Ground spices lose aroma. We print the mill date and recommend use within 90 days of that date, stored sealed, cool, and dry, away from sunlight. We do not bid a longer shelf-life claim.

## 5. How the order is produced and delivered

1. The buyer sends a written order (paper, email, or message) with product, kilograms, delivery address, contact phone, and requested date.
2. We confirm the order, the total in birr, and the delivery day before we mill.
3. We mill the batch for that order, or draw from a batch milled for the same product if it is still inside the 90-day window and the quality check in section 4.4 passes.
4. We pack in food-grade bags of 1 kg, or in 5 kg bags when the order line is at least 5 kg and the buyer asks for that pack size.
5. We deliver in Addis Ababa, or to a nearby city when that leg was priced in the order confirmation.

**Lead time after the order is confirmed in writing**

| Situation | Delivery |
| --- | --- |
| Goods already milled and in stock | Next working day inside Addis Ababa |
| Milled for the order, up to 100 kg total | Within 5 working days |
| Above 100 kg in one order, or outside Addis Ababa | Date stated in the order confirmation before we accept |

Do not promise a named calendar date in the letter until you know stock and the buyer’s quantities. The table above is the standing offer.

**Delivery charge**

- Inside Addis Ababa, delivery is included when the order total is at least 2,000 ETB.
- Inside Addis Ababa, below 2,000 ETB, we quote the delivery cost before dispatch and add it only if the buyer agrees.
- Outside Addis Ababa, delivery is quoted with the order and is not included in the unit rates.

## 7. Commercial terms

1. **Currency.** Ethiopian birr.
2. **Unit.** Kilogram, net weight.
3. **Rates.** The rates in Annex A, firm for 60 days from the letter date. A rate change after that date applies only to orders confirmed later.
4. **Minimum.** 1 kg per product line.
5. **Acceptance.** A written confirmation from Taem Baltina forms the contract for that order. This bid alone does not oblige us to supply unlimited quantity.
6. **Capacity.** Orders above 100 kg need a confirmed date, as in section 5.
7. **Title and risk.** Risk passes when the buyer or the named receiver signs the delivery note. Until that signature, risk stays with Taem Baltina.
8. **Rejected goods.** Section 4.4. We collect rejected packs at our cost inside Addis Ababa.
9. **No substitution.** We do not substitute mitmita for berbere, or a different blend, unless the buyer agrees in writing.
10. **Samples.** On request, we provide up to 100 g of each product bid, against the buyer’s written sample request. Sample weight is not billed if the buyer places an order within the bid validity. Otherwise the sample is billed at the Annex A rate.
11. **Law.** The order is performed in Addis Ababa. Disputes that we cannot settle by discussion are referred to the competent Ethiopian court in Addis Ababa.
